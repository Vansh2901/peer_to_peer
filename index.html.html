Project objectives

What this portal is built to achieve.

  1. 1

    Provide a centralized web app to upload, search and request textbooks, reference materials and semester notes.

  2. 2

    Implement secure authentication and profiles so only verified college students can exchange materials.

  3. 3

    Promote campus sustainability by reusing physical books and reducing paper through digital note sharing.

  4. 4

    Offer category-based filtering and search by branch, semester and subject.

  5. 5

    Establish a peer-to-peer notification system connecting seekers with students who can donate or lend.
